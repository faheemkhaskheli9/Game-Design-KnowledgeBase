# Glossary

Terms used across the base, each linked to the topic that explains it.

- **MDA** - Mechanics, Dynamics, Aesthetics: designers build mechanics, players experience aesthetics. [[what-makes-a-game-fun]]
- **Core loop** - the repeated cycle of actions and rewards at the heart of a game. [[core-gameplay-loop]]
- **Flow** - absorbed state when challenge matches skill. [[flow-and-difficulty-curves]]
- **Dynamic difficulty adjustment (DDA)** - changing difficulty at runtime from player performance. [[flow-and-difficulty-curves]]
- **Self-Determination Theory (SDT)** - motivation from competence, autonomy and relatedness. [[player-motivation-and-player-types]]
- **Overjustification effect** - extrinsic rewards crowding out intrinsic enjoyment. [[player-motivation-and-player-types]]
- **Interesting decision** - a choice with trade-offs and no obvious best answer. [[meaningful-choices]]
- **Dominant strategy** - an option that is best in all situations, collapsing choice. [[game-balance]]
- **Intransitive balance** - rock-paper-scissors relations where nothing beats everything. [[game-balance]]
- **Cost curve** - the mapping from an option's cost to the power it should provide. [[game-balance]]
- **Snowballing** - a positive feedback loop where an early lead grows. [[feedback-loops-and-snowballing]]
- **Rubber banding** - a negative feedback loop that helps players who are behind. [[feedback-loops-and-snowballing]]
- **Input/output randomness** - randomness before the player decides vs after. [[randomness-and-luck]]
- **Pity timer** - guaranteed reward after a run of bad luck. [[randomness-and-luck]]
- **Telegraphing** - visibly signalling an attack before it lands. [[combat-and-encounter-design]]
- **Vertical/horizontal progression** - more power vs more options. [[progression-systems]]
- **Meta-progression** - persistent progress across runs in roguelites. [[progression-systems]]
- **Variable ratio schedule** - rewards after an unpredictable number of actions. [[reward-schedules-and-loot]]
- **Faucet and sink** - where currency enters and leaves an economy. [[game-economy-sources-and-sinks]]
- **Dark pattern** - design that exploits players against their interests. [[retention-and-session-design]]
- **Juice** - extra feedback (shake, particles, sound) that makes actions feel good. [[game-feel-and-juice]]
- **Coyote time** - a short grace period to jump after leaving a ledge. [[game-feel-and-juice]]
- **Kishotenketsu** - four-step level structure: introduce, develop, twist, conclude. [[level-design-and-pacing]]
- **Ludonarrative dissonance** - conflict between what the story says and what the mechanics reward. [[narrative-and-player-agency]]
- **Elo rating** - skill rating updated from expected vs actual results. [[multiplayer-and-social-play]]
- **Vertical slice** - a small, fully polished section that proves the final experience. [[prototyping-and-finding-the-fun]]
- **Think-aloud playtest** - players narrate their thoughts while the designer watches silently. [[playtesting-and-metrics]]
